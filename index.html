Build your own pixel realm and play it with friends

Sparkia is a cozy online sandbox. Break and place blocks, grow trees from seeds and trade with other players.

Build a realm of your own

Every realm starts as wild land. Punch blocks to break them, place your own, and put up doors and signs.

Place a Realm Anchor to claim it as yours, then give friends access so they can build with you.

Grow everything from seeds

Plant seeds, grow trees and harvest them for blocks and more seeds. Splice two seeds together to discover new ones.

Rarer blocks take longer to grow. Learn farming skills on your skill tree to farm faster and open Farmer's Packs.

Trade with other players

Swap items face to face in the trade window. Spark Cubes are the currency players trade with.

Earn Sparks as you play and spend them in the Sparkia Store on packs, things to wear and a bigger inventory.
